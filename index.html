Formula Count and Source Notes

The reference index lists 77 numbered equations: 42 drilling and 35 blasting. Search covers 90 formula and result cards, including simulator outputs and grouped equations.

  • U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Engineering and Design: Blasting for Rock Excavations (EM 1110-2-3800) — blasting design methods, including burden guidance.
  • U.S. Bureau of Mines, RI 7653: Ground Vibrations from Tunnel Blasting in Granite — an empirical vibration study whose fitted coefficients depend on the site and blast conditions.
  • International Society of Explosives Engineers (ISEE): Blasters' Handbook (18th Edition) — Initiation timing, non-electric shock tube delay sequencing, and electrical blasting circuit Ohm's verification.
  • DuPont de Nemours: Blaster's Handbook — Standards for electric cap firing current thresholds (1.5 A DC minimum), balanced parallel-series network design, and lead-wire resistance charts.
  • Dyno Nobel & Orica Mining Services: Technical Information Handbooks — Specifications for NONEL tube propagation velocity (2,000 m/s), detonating cord PETN loading, and safe delay timing intervals.

These references support selected blasting methods; the original source for each drilling and thermochemistry equation is not identified in this file, so those equations still need individual source attribution.
